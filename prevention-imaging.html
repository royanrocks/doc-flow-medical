Prevention & Imaging

Prevention is the foundation of long-term health. We combine advanced diagnostics and imaging referrals with proactive screening schedules tailored to your age, history, and risk factors.

Preventive Screenings

  • Cardiovascular and metabolic risk assessment
  • Cancer screening coordination (colonoscopy, mammography, low-dose CT)
  • Bone density and osteoporosis screening
  • Advanced lipid and biomarker panels

Imaging

We coordinate advanced imaging with trusted partners and review every result with you personally, in plain language — English or Spanish — so you always understand what your results mean and what comes next.
